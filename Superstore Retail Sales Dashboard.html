Key Findings

  • Sales and profit show noticeable fluctuations over time.
  • Technology has the highest total sales among the categories.
  • Sales are positively skewed, with some very high-value transactions.
  • Higher discounts are associated with greater variation in profit, including negative-profit transactions.
  • The correlation heatmap shows relationships among the key numerical variables.

Conclusion

The project successfully demonstrates data inspection, cleaning, feature engineering, statistical analysis, and exploratory data visualization to identify meaningful patterns in retail sales data.
